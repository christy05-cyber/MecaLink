About MecaLink

MecaLink is a platform designed to connect vehicle owners with mechanics, automotive services and spare parts.

Our goal is to make it easier for people to find help when they experience vehicle problems and to connect mechanics and automotive sellers with potential customers.

Our Mission

To create a simple and accessible platform that connects people with reliable automotive assistance and services.
